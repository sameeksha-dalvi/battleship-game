How to Play

  • Each player has a fleet of ships placed on a 10×10 grid.
  • Your ships are visible on your board.
  • Enemy ships are hidden.
  • Click a cell on the enemy board to fire a shot.
  • Hit
  • Miss
  • Players take turns attacking.
  • Sink all enemy ships to win the game.
